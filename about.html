About MANĀR

Law does not exist apart from the societies that produce it, the histories it inherits, or the forms of power through which it operates.

MANĀR is an independent publication examining the relationship between law, political thought, and social life. Grounded in South Asia and attentive to wider intellectual traditions, MANĀR brings legal inquiry into conversation with history, philosophy, anthropology, religion, literature, and the social sciences.

Article Formats & Submission Guidelines

Essays (1,000–2,000 words)

Clear arguments about legal, political, historical, or philosophical questions.

Legal Analysis (1,500–3,000 words)

Examination of legislation, judgments, legal doctrines, and public policy.

Long Reads (2,500–4,500 words)

Sustained investigations requiring deeper historical or theoretical analysis.

Research Papers (4,000–8,000 words)

Substantial original research with engagement in primary scholarship.
